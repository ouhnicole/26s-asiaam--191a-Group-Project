# Pinning UCLA Pilipinx Students’ ICE Experiences and Campus Resources
An online mapping application that provides UCLA Samahang Pilipino students a platform to anonymously share their experiences with how ICE Enforcements of a loved one has affected their lives. Community members can use the interactive map and testimonies to better understand the broader impacts of immigration enforcement while recognizing shared experiences and strengthening community understanding.

## Objective
Our project aims to demonstrate how the impacts of ICE detentions extend beyond those directly affected who were either detained or deported. As our primary target organization includes the UCLA Samahang Pilipino community, our mapping application focuses on UCLA Filipinx students by collecting anonymous responses about whether someone close to them has been detained or deported by ICE and how that experience shaped their lives so far. Instead of solely focusing on the number of ICE detentions itself, our project plans to analyze the repercussions that follow: emotional distress, financial strain, physical and mental health, and everyday challenges. By visualizing these experiences in a mapping application, our group aims to help Samahang Pilipino identify shared recurring experiences, highlighting the consequences and challenges faced by communities affected by ICE enforcements.

## Who is being empowered?
Our website empowers the UCLA Pilipinx community by giving them a safe, anonymous space to share their personal experiences with deportation and ICE detention. Instead of reducing these experiences to statistics, the application visualizes anonymous testimonies on an interactive map, allowing users to recognize patterns across different hometowns, family backgrounds, and lived experiences while identifying gaps in UCLA campus resources and support services to help organizations such as Samahang Pilipino better advocate for students with shared experiences. By reading others' stories, students can see they are not alone, feel represented, and develop a stronger sense of solidarity within Samahang Pilipino. At the same time, the collected testimonies can help the organization identify recurring challenges and better understand where additional resources and support may be needed. 

## Technology Stack

## How can it be repurposed?
For the future, we hope to incorporate more feedback from other members of the Pilipinx community as well as those involved in the UCLA Samahang Pilipino so the application reflects the priorities and lived experiences of the community it is designed to serve. These improvements will help create a website that is not only functional but also more representative, trustworthy, and useful for future advocacy and community support. Although our mapplication currently focuses on UCLA Piliipinx students, we designed it to be adaptable. In the future, it could be expanded to other immigrant communities, universities, or organizations that want to document how ICE enforcement affects their community. By partnering with other community organizations, our mapplication can help improve support services while identifying common needs and long term impacts experienced by different communities. We also foresee it becoming a valuable public resource for research and public education to help provide community centered perspectives on ICE enforcement. Since the application is built around anonymous testimonies and interactive mapping, it could also be repurposed to document other community issues like discrimination, housing insecurity, and access to mental health resources. This will allow organizations to better understand and respond to the needs of the communities.
