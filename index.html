Beyond Immigration: UCLA Pilipinx Response Map

This map highlights anonymous responses from UCLA Pilipinx students on whether they believe UCLA provides adequate resources for students affected by the deportation of a loved one. Each response offers insight into students' perspectives on campus support and the lasting impacts of deportation on their lives and communities.
