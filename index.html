Beyond Immigration

Mapping the ICE experiences of UCLA Pilipinx students through anonymous stories and community-centered data. We prioritize safety, representation, and respectful design.

Our Mission

Center UCLA Pilipinx students' stories. We prioritize anonymity, community control, and accessible design so people can safely share experiences.
